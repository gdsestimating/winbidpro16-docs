WinBidPro will only calculate labor when you apply **Labor Rules** to your elevations. A single labor rule describes a type of task and how the time is added up for that task. 

Multiple labor rules can be grouped into a reusable list called a **Labor Ruleset**. Once a labor ruleset is defined, you can easily apply that ruleset to any elevation to have WinBidPro calculate the labor for it. 

## Configuring Labor

Labor can be configured at the job-level, or across all your new jobs at the organizational level.

    * To configure labor for your current job, go to `Job` > `Labor Rules & Settings` in the main menu. This opens the `Job Labor` window.

    * To configure labor for your organization, go to `Settings` > `Labor Settings` in the main menu.

Once your labor rulesets are configured, you'll apply it to your elevations. You can either do this as you create the elevation by using the `Labor Ruleset` dropdown, or you can set it in the `Job Labor` window by clicking one or more elevations and selecting a ruleset from the dropdown menu.

:::warning
    WinBidPro provides a number of example rulesets by default, but these should not be used for estimates until they are reviewed and modified. 
:::

## Labor Rules

With an elevation selected in the `Job Labor` window, you can add individual rules to that elevation by clicking `+Add Line Item`. Rules added like this won't change the ruleset applied and will use **bold** text to mark it as a manually added rule.

### Configuring Labor Rules



* `Name` - A unique name for this rule.

* `Category` - The category of labor determining the base rate of the task. 

    * You can set the rates for each of these in the `Rates & Settings` tab, see below.
    
* `How Applied` - Defines how a task is calculated from the conditions found in your elevations.
    
    * Options include things like `Per Joint` or `Per foot of Elevation Perimeter`.
    
* `Minutes` - Estimated time it would take to complete the task.

:::tip
    The `Per Lite per Glazier` setting in `How Applied` takes into account both the number of lites and how many glaziers are required based on the weight of the glass lite. 

    Weight thresholds used for this option are set in the `Rates & Settings` tab and are based on the make up of the `Glass Products` applied to your elevations.
:::


### Configuring Rulesets

Rulesets can be added or edited at the organization (global) level through the `Settings` > `Labor Settings` window, or on the job level by clicking `Edit Labor Ruleset...` in the `Job Labor` window.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/labor-rulesets.png"/></div>



Start by naming the ruleset, then add individual rules to it. Click `Save` to confirm, or `Cancel` to cancel.

If you edit a global ruleset in the `Job Labor` window, it will make a copy of that ruleset that you can edit without effecting the global version. You can save your job ruleset to your global rulesets by clicking `Save to Global Rulesets`.

## Rates & Settings


<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/labor-settings.png"/></div>

**Glazing Labor Defaults**

* `Max Weight Lifted Per Worker` - Threshold for weight in pounds for the amount of workers needed for a task by.

* `Threshold at which Equipment is Required` - Threshold for weight in pounds for when special equipment is needed to complete a task.

* `Workers needed when using Equipment` - Amount of workers needed once equipment threshold is met.

**Labor Rates** - Rates for different labor categories. Costs are assigned here and are applied by category as part of the `Labor Rules`. Categories include:

* `Fab Rate`

* `Install Rate`

* `Glazing Rate`

* `Misc Rate`

### Rules & Rates Example

:::info
    **Example**
    
    The Panel Fab rule has has these values: 
    
    * `Category`: Fabrication
        * Fabrication Rate is $40.00/hr (found in the `Rates & Settings` tab)
    * `How Applied`: Per Panel
    * `Minutes`: 10


    So if your elevation has 12 panels in it, applying this rule to your elevation will add 120 minutes to the job and $80.00 to your cost estimate.
:::

## Labor Breakdown

The `Labor Breakdown` tab of the `Job Labor` window gives you an overview of all labor applied in your current job.

This tab shows a line for each rule as it applies to each elevation, how many times they're applied based on the elevation quantities, and a summary of all the rule options, their calculations, and the total time for each task.

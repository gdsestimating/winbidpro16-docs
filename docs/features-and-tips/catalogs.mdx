## Catalogs

At the core of WinBidPro are **`Framing Systems`** and **`Parts`**. These are digital versions of the parts, hardware, assemblies, and installation rules that make up your glazed openings. These are collected into **`Catalogs`**.





### Vendor Catalogs

When you first launch WinBidPro, you'll be asked what `Vendor Catalogs` to add to your organization. These are preconfigured catalogs made by GDS, and reflect many of the most requested options from users. 

:::info
    Due to licensing and legal agreements, some preconfigured vendors do not have pricing assigned to their `Vendor Catalog`.
:::

You can add or remove vendors from your team's available `catalogs` by clicking `Catalogs`>`Manage Vendors...` in the main menu.

### Requests 

You can request new additions `catalog` by clicking `Help`>`Catalog Add/Edit Request` in the main menu.

If you notice that a system or its pricing has recently changed and needs to be updated, you can also use this option to help inform us.

:::note
    Please note requests are prioritized based on our availability and how often they get requested.

    For any immediate need, see `Editing Catalogs` below.
:::

### Editing Catalogs
WinBidPro has an extensive set of features to configure your own parts, systems, and `catalogs`. Once they're correctly configured, you can use them just like the preconfigured ones.

If there's something you need from a specific vendor that's not in a vendor's preconfigured list, these can be added by you on a per-job basis, or directly to your team's vendor catalog. We'll go into more detail on what that means in the next article - [Core Concepts 3 - Local and Global Edits](./config-levels.mdx).

* See the [Custom Parts & Systems](../custom-parts) section to learn more about configuring your own parts and systems.

You can create a completely new catalog by clicking `Create` in the top right corner of the `Manage Vendors` window, see image above.


### Important Considerations
* `Catalogs` are shared by your team. Make sure you make changes carefully, it might not be obvious to others when something's been changed.


## Parts, Systems, and More

Your `catalogs` are broken up into these categories:

* Vendor Specific
    * `Finishes` - Standard finish options.
    * `Parts` - List of part numbers and data for individual parts.
    * `Framing Systems` - List of configured systems, each with part assemblies, settings, and installation logic for accurate counts.
    * `Entrances` - Preset combinations of `Door Frames` and `Door Leafs`.
        * `Door Frames` - Part assemblies with special logic for framing a door opening.
        * `Door Leafs` - Special `Part` for use in `Door Frames`.
* Independent
    * `Glass Table` - Glass product details that get assigned to `panels` in your `elevations`.
    * `Operable Window Templates` - Operable window details that get assigned to `panels` in your `elevations`.

See the [Drawing Elevations](../drawing-elevations/) section for more details on how to apply each of these.

See the [Custom Parts & Systems](../custom-parts) section to learn more about configuring your own parts and systems.

### Components & Assemblies

`Framing Systems`, `Door Frames`, and some other areas of WinBidPro use **`Components`** and **`Assemblies`**.

`Components` are collections of parts paired wit additional logic for accurate estimating, like spacing for screws. `Assemblies` are a type of `component` that bring together a list of parts, usually one or more extrusions, and hardware.

These topics get covered in depth in the [Custom Parts & Systems](../custom-parts) section, but the important thing to know is that these are just terms for `parts` and lists of `parts` when they get referenced in other `catalog` entries.

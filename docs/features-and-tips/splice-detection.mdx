Elevations drawn with frames lengths longer than the stock length (minus clean up cuts) of a framing systems' configured parts will trigger a splice detection window to pop up.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/splice-detect.png"/></div>

## Options

* `Automatically Apply Splices At Stock Lengths` - Quicker estimating. Allow the app to automatically apply splices where they are needed. These splices can be adjusted or deleted in the [Splice tool tab](../drawing-elevations/splices) once they are applied.

* `I Will Add Splices Later` - Better Accuracy. Configure your own splices later using the [Splice tool tab](../drawing-elevations/splices). 

    * Until these are resolved, [Job Alerts](./job-alerts) will appear for all required splices.

## Settings
* ☑ `Remember this choice for this Job` - Toggle whether this choice applies to just this elevation or all elevation in this job.

* ☑ `Remember this choice for new Jobs` - Toggle whether this choice applies to just this job or all jobs moving forward.

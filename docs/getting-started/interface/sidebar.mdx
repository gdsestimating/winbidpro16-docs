### Job Explorer<img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/job-explorer.png"/>

A summary of your current job. 

* `Job Explorer` includes:

    * All elevations in the job.

    * `Data Tables` - List of local copies of systems, glass and labor loaded into your job.

        * See [Local and Global Edits](../concepts/config-levels) to learn how these differ from the catalog versions.

* You can right-click each line in the job explorer for more options.



<br clear="right"/>

### Reports Explorer<img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/reports-explorer.png"/>

A configurable menu for quick-access to `Reports`. 

* <img src="/screenshots/interface/report-gear.png"/> `Settings` - Opens [`All Reports and Settings`](../../output/reports#all-reports--settings) window.

* `Search` - Find specific reports in the list.

* <img src="/screenshots/interface/report-pin.png"/> `Pin` - Pin / unpin this report to the top of the list.




<br clear="right"/>

### Elevation Totals<img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/elevation-totals.png"/>

A list of estimate totals for the current elevation that updates as you make changes. 

This shows running totals of:
* Counts & Cuts 
* Measurements
* Joints
* Labor

<br clear="right"/>

## Layer Settings<img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/layer-settings.png"/>

Configure graphic settings for your elevations, broken out into sections like `Frame` or `Glass`.

* `Configuration` - Use this section to save custom presets and switch between them.

* `Search` - Find a specific layer setting.

* For most linework you can:

    * Switch between `Visible`, `Hidden`, or `Excluded from Export`. 

    * Switch the default layer color by clicking the color swatch next to the toggle options.

    * You can also change the fill pattern for panel elements, like `Glass` and `Operable Windows`.

* `Dimensions` - Configure visibility and color for all dimensions, and toggle on / off specific types of dimensions.
    
    * This is also where you set your dimension styles, scale, format, and orientation.

* `Displayed Text` - Change the format of non-dimension annotations and dimension subtext for things like rough openings.

* `Selections` - Change how things look when selected or hovered over.

* `Misc` - Change the background color of the elevation view.

* `Editor Settings` - Open the editor settings menu. Also can be found under `Settings`  in the main menu.

* `Reset to Default` - Reset all settings back to WinBidPro defaults.

## Lists

* `Scroll` - While your mouse is over a list, you can use your mouse wheel to scroll up and down through it.

* `Right-click` - In some cases like the `Job Explorer` sidebar, you can right-click in the empty space of the window for quick options.

## CAD Views

* `Zoom` - Use your scroll wheel to zoom in and out in a CAD view.
* `Pan` - Hold right-click and drag to pan around in a CAD view.

### View Tools

<img src="/screenshots/ViewerTools.png"/><br/>

CAD views have these options in their corners. From left to right, these include:

* `Measure` - See Measurement section below for more details.

* `Zoom to Fit` - Zoom to fit the entire CAD drawing in the view.
* `Zoom Window` - Click-and-drag a selection box to zoom to a specific area.
* `Zoom` - Toggles click-and-drag zoom. Left-click your mouse and drag it away from you to zoom in, and towards you to zoom out.
* `Pan` - Toggles click-and-drag panning for left-click.

### Measurement

Most CAD views in WinBidPro give you access to a measurement tool (see View Tools above). If you click on the measurement tool icon, you'll switch to measuring mode. You can hit `Esc` at any point to leave this mode. 

Once your first point is placed, you'll see a `+` at that point and some live details and options in the bottom left of the CAD window. When you click at least two points, the tool will draw a line between them and the length of that line will be labelled

While the measuring tool is active, point-snapping is also active (see Point-Snap below).



<img src="/screenshots/interface/measure-stats.png"/><br/>




#### Live Information

Gives you live details of your measurement as the points are placed, or as you move your mouse across the CAD view.

    * `Length` - Displays the length between the last point and the current location of your cursor.
    * `Total` - Displays the total of all measures so far, not including the distance from the last placed point to your cursor.
    * `Angle` - Displays the angle between the last point and the current location of your cursor.

#### Options

    * `Esc` - Undo the last placed point.
    * `C` - Copy the `Total` to your clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere.
    * `D` - Change the angle units from degrees to radians.
    * `Hold Z` - Lock your mouse to horizontal movement only.
    * `Hold X` - Lock your mouse to vertical movement only.
    * `V` - Toggle `Length` and `Total` labels near your cursor.
    * `B` - Toggle point coordinate labels. 

    :::info
        **Point Coordinates** are measured in inches relative to the **origin**. The **origin** will read as `X = 0", Y = 0"` is the bottom left corner of your elevation. 
        
        * The `X` value is the horizontal distance from the origin, in inches. 
        * The `Y` value is the vertical distance from the origin, in inches.
        
        Where bulkheads are applied, the origin is where the left-most part of your frame and the bottom-most part of your frame would intersect. 
    ::: 

 

### Point-Snap

Some tools in WinBidPro, like rakes or the measurement tool, will snap to specific points on the elevation by default. These are the `▢` endpoints and `△` midpoints of parts in your assemblies.

* Snap points will show up as green squares and triangles while the tool is active.

* When you get close to a point, it will switch to yellow. 

    * If you left-click while they're yellow, the tool with automatically set the first point to that point.

* You can temporarily disable point-snapping by holding the `CTRL` key while.

### Selecting Elements & Panels

Some options will ask you to select one or more panels or elements. When you click those elements or panels, you’ll see them highlighted in green. For panels, you'll also see green check icon.

In many cases, a set of quick selection tools in the bottom left corner of the previewer.

* In this example, these buttons will select `All` panels, `Odd` or `Even` numbered panels, or deselect all panels with `None`.

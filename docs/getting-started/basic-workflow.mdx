## 1. Create a New Job

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/sample-new-job.png"/></div>

1. Click `Job` in the tool bar and select `New Job`, or press `Ctrl + Shift + N`.

2. Select a vendor [Catalog](../features-and-tips/catalogs), type in a job name, and select a default finish option.

    * Jobs in WinBidPro can only have one vendor catalog assigned.

    * For more advanced users, vendor catalogs can be refined, customized, or even made from scratch right in WinBidPro. See [Custom Parts & Systems](../custom-parts/) for more details.

    * The default finish option helps save a step in the future, you can always pick a different finish when creating elevations. 

3. Create your first elevation, see below.

## 2. Create Elevations

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/sample-new-elevation.png"/></div>

1. Create your first [Elevation](../drawing-elevations/) and give it an Elevation Type, Mark, Framing System, Finish and Labor Ruleset. You can also chose to give it a Description to identify it more easily.

2. Draw your elevation using the tool tabs at the bottom of your screen. 
    
    * To start, you'll need at least an `Original Width`, `Original Height` and a number of `Panels` before the elevation can be previewed. Once there's a preview, the other tool tabs will become available.

        * Refine the elevation using the tool tabs until it matches your drawing set. See the tool tabs under [Drawing Elevations](../drawing-elevations/) for more details.

3. Create new elevations by clicking `New Elevations` in the `Job` toolbar menu, or press `Ctrl`+`N`.

4. Continue creating and drawing elevations until you have all the ones you need.
 

## 3. Review Parts Lists and Pricing

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/sample-optimizer.png"/></div>

As you draw your elevations, your parts lists and totals are being updated automatically. The next step is to review and refine these lists to [Finalize Your Estimate](../finalize/).

You can find these summaries, and your estimate settings, in the top half of `Reports` in the toolbar. These are shown with a black icon.

1. Review the results, set multipliers, and set markups and labor rules to refine your estimate as needed.

2. Once you're comfortable with your elevations and your reports, you're ready to export.

## 4. Export the Job

1. Export your outputs as needed. 

    * Exports fall under two categories, [printed reports](../output/reports) and [shop drawings](../output/shop-drawings):

### Printed Reports

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/sample-printed-report.png"/></div>

You can find printed reports under the `Reports` toolbar. Printed reports are shown with green icon. These cover your lists, spreadsheets, labels and job summaries to be shared outside of WinBidPro.

### Shop Drawings

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/sample-shop-drawings.png"/></div>

Click `Drawings` in the toolbar to find options related to shop drawings:

Here you can configure a [cover page](../output/cover-page), and generate shop drawings with a configurable [title block](../output/title-blocks). Generated shop drawings will include each of your elevations, and any details included in the configured framing system. 

Once the drawings are generated, you can also export them to .DWG or .DXF files for use in CAD programs.

Add splices to vertical/horizontal elements of your elevation.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/tool-tabs/editor-splice-3.png"/></div>

The Splices Tool Tab will show a list of splices applied to the frames in your current elevation. You can use this list to quickly review, edit, or delete existing splices.

A preview of your splice cuts will appear in the elevation using white lines by default.

If splices haven't been added to this elevation, the list will show `No Splices`.

## Splice Options

* `Add` - Manually add a new splice at a specific point on a vertical or horizontal element. See below for more details.
* `Auto Apply Vertical` - Automatically add splices at full stock lengths on applicable verticals.
* `Auto Apply Receptor` - Automatically add splices at full stock lengths on applicable head and sill receptors.

Any automatically applied splices will be added to the splice list where you can edit or delete them as needed. 

## Adding & Editing Splices

For the `Add` option, you'll be brought to a new menu.

:::tip
    The Splice tool for manually adding a splice uses point-snapping to automatically place your mouse at end points and mid points of frame segments as you get close to them.

    You can temporarily disable this by holding `Ctrl` while you're placing points.
:::

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/tool-tabs/editor-splice-2.png"/></div>

### Storefront Types & Options

* `Verticals`
    * `Verticals to Splice` - Either select the verticals in the viewer or type in their label 
    * ☑ `Back Member` - Toggle to apply the splice to back members
    * `Position` - Where the splice is applied, measured in inches from the bottom of the elevation

*  `Receptors / Sub Sill`
Horizontal splice for Head and Sill Receptors / Sub Sills

    * `What to Splice` - Toggles for ☑ `Head Receptor` and ☑ `Sill Receptor / Subsill`
    * `Where to Splice` - Select a snap point to place a splice or toggle ☑ `Measured Splice` to set a splice point measured in inches from the leftmost point on the elevation

*  `Head and Sill`
Horizontal splice for Heads and Sills

    * `What to Splice` - Toggles for ☑ `Head`, ☑ `Sill` and ☑ `Back Member`
    * `Where to Splice` - Select a snap point to place a splice or toggle ☑ `Measured Splice` to set a splice point measured in inches from the leftmost point on the elevation

### Curtain Wall Types & Options

* `Verticals`
    * `Verticals to Splice` - Either select the verticals in the viewer or type in their label.
        * Below this are toggles to apply this splice to the ☑ `Face Cap`, ☑ `Pressure Plate`, and/or ☑ `Back Member` where applicable.
    * `Position` - Where the splice is applied, measured in inches from the bottom of the elevation.

*  `Receptors / Sub Sill` - Horizontal splice for Head and Sill Receptors / Sub Sills

    * `What to Splice` - Toggles for ☑ `Head Receptor` and ☑ `Sill Receptor / Subsill`
    * `Where to Splice` - Select a snap point to place a splice or toggle ☑ `Measured Splice` to set a splice point measured in inches from the leftmost point on the elevation

*  `Head and Sill` - Horizontal splice for Heads and Sills

    * `What to Splice` - Toggles for ☑ `Head`, ☑ `Sill` and ☑ `Back Member`
    * `Where to Splice` - Select a snap point to place a splice or toggle ☑ `Measured Splice` to set a splice point measured in inches from the leftmost point on the elevation

### Confirm / Return

Click `Save` to confirm your settings and apply the splice.

Click `Back` to return to the list view.

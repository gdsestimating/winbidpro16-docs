Optimizers are you part lists, cut lists, products, labor settings, etc. in WinBidPro. These are the black icons in the `Reports` toolbar menu.

For printing reports, reports and labels not intended for collaborators not using WinBidPro, see [Printed Reports](/docs/v16/output/reports). These reports use a green icon in the `Reports` toolbar menu.

## Doors
An list of entrances in the current job, offering a searchable and filterable spreadsheet of all entrances.

By default, this window is opened as an attached Job Window in the interface.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/doors-report.png"/></div>

## Elevations Overview
An expanded version of the list of elevations in the current job found in the Job Explorer, offering a searchable and filterable spreadsheet of all the elevations and their more important characteristics.

By default, this window is opened as an attached Job Window in the interface.

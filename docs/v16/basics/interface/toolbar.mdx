## Job Menu <img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/toolbar-job.png"/>



Manage and review your current job, or start / open another one.

* `New Job` - Start a new job.
* `Open Job` - Open existing jobs from your team's account.
* `New Elevation` - Create a new [Elevation](/docs/v16/jobs/elevations) in the current job.
* `Import Elevations` - Import elevations from other exports and third-party resources.

### Edit Job

* `Job Details` - Edit job details like Status, Bid Date, Contacts, and job-specific settings.
* `Parts` - Manage the list of parts used in the current job.
* `Glass` - Manage the list of glass products used in the current job.
* `Labor Rules & Settings` - Manage [Labor Rules and Settings](/docs/v16/configuration/labor-rules) for the current job.
* `Finishes` - Manage the list of finishes available for the current job.

### Manage Job

* `View Job Alerts` - Open the [Job Alerts](/docs/v16/jobs/job-alerts.mdx) window.
* `Lock Job` - Prevent any changes from being made to the current job.
* `Close Job` - Close the current job

## Reports Menu <img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/toolbar-reports.png"/>

Open job summaries, optimizers and exported reports for the current job. 

A breakdown of each report type will appear on the following pages:

* [`Non-Printed Reports`](/docs/v16/jobs/summaries/summaries) for the black icons found in this menu or,
* [`Printed Reports`](/docs/v16/output/reports) for the green icons.

`Export Reports Data` allows you to export all information contained in this job. 
* See [What's In Our Exports](../it-dev/exports) for more details.

<br clear="right"/>

## Drawings Menu <img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/toolbar-drawings.png"/>


Manage and export drawings on the current job and your team's Cover Page.

* `Shop Drawings` - Configure [Shop Drawings](/docs/v16/output/shop-drawings) for the current job.
* `Cover Page` - Manage your team's [Cover Page](/docs/v16/output/cover-page) for printed drawing sets.
* `Irregular Glass` - Configure drawings for irregular shaped glass in the current job.
* `Export Drawings to DWG/DXF` - Generate CAD files of drawings in the current job.
* `Refresh Active Drawing` - Force a refresh of all generated drawings.


<br clear="right"/>

## Catalogs Menu <img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/toolbar-catalog.png"/>

Set up and manage your team's catalogs.

* `View Available Catalogs` - View vendor catalogs tracked by your team.
* `Manage Vendors` - Manage vendors from your team's list of catalogs.

### Active Catalog

Manage the `Parts`, `Systems`, `CAD Details`, etc. for the catalog selected for your current job.
    * See the [Configuration](/docs/v16/configuration/configuration) section for more details.

### Products & Templates

* `Glass Table` - Manage [Glass Products](/docs/v16/configuration/parts/glass-products) available to your team.
* `Part Types` - Manage part types available to your team.
* `Operable Window Templates` - Manage [Operable Window Templates](/docs/v16/configuration/parts/window-template) available to your team.
* `Contact List` - Manage your team's contact list.

<br clear="right"/>

## Settings Menu <img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/toolbar-settings.png"/>


Set up and manage your settings and your team's settings for all jobs.

* `Labor Settings` - Set global and default settings for labor pricing.
* `Editor Settings` - Change your default app settings and appearance.

### Team Settings

* `Team Editor Settings` - Set team-wide operational settings like blade thickness for cuts.
* `Link To Team` - Link between different teams using GDS.

<br clear="right"/>

## View Menu <img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/toolbar-view.png"/>

Toggle your visible windows and reset your layout to default.

* `Open Welcome Tab` - Opens the Welcome tab window in your workspace.
* ☑ `Show Welcome Tab on Startup` - Toggles showing the welcome tab every time you load the app.

### Main Window Layout

* ☑ `Show Tool Tabs` - Toggle the tools tabs interface at the bottom of your screen.
* ☑ `Show Layer Settings` - Toggle the layer settings window.
* ☑ `Show Elevation Totals` - Toggle the Elevation Totals window.
* ☑ `Show Reports Explorer` - Toggle the Reports Explorer window

### Layout Resets

See [Setting Up Your Workspace](./workspace-config) for more details.

* `Reset Docked Panels Layout` - Resets all docked locations to the default layout.
* `Reset Window Sizes & Positions` - Resets all window sizes and positions to the default settings.

<br clear="right"/>

## Help Menu <img align="right" src="/screenshots/interface/toolbar-help.png"/>


Find documentation, submit issues and requests, and get help from GDS.

* `Request / Vote Features` - Click to open our feature request dashboard.
* `Support / Bug Report` - Submit a support request or report an issue.
* `Catalog Add / Edit Request` - Submit a request to edit or add systems and parts to our preconfigured vendor catalogs. 
* `Open Training Videos` - Click to open our training videos.
* `Open Documentation` - Click to open our documentation.
* `Open Contextual Help` - Open a contextual help window that will give you more information on the tools you're currently using.
* `GDS Screen Connect` - A support feature to help us connect to your WinBidPro session when you're having issues.
* `About` - Details on which WinBidPro version you're using and links our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Add a new entrance/door to your elevation at selected panels.

This tab lists all applied entrances in this elevation with buttons to delete them or edit their frames and leaves. If entrances haven't been added, the list will show `No Entrances` instead.

A preview of your added entrances will appear in your elevation.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/tool-tabs/editor-door-3.png"/></div>



## Adding Entrances

Click the `Add` button to create a new entrance.

Your tab will switch to a new view where you can add entrances by selecting panels and setting your entrance options.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/tool-tabs/editor-door-2.png"/></div>

### Options

* `Add Door Frame` - `Browse` your current vendor catalog for a pre-configured door frame, or `Edit` a previous choice. 
    * Once you have one selected the option to edit the door frame becomes available.

* `Add Door Leaf` - `Browse` your current vendor catalog for a pre-configured door leaf, or `Edit` a previous choice.  
* `Select Existing` - `Browse` your current vendor catalog for a pre-configured frame & leaf assembly, or select one currently in use `From Job`.

### Confirm / Return

Click `Save` to confirm your settings, apply the entrance to the elevation, and return to the list view.

Click `Cancel` to cancel, and return to the list view.

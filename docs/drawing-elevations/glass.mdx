Apply glass products to selected panels.

The Glass Tool Tab will show a list of glass products loaded on the current job. When these products are applied to an elevation, the panels will be updated with type labels.

If glass products haven't been added to this job, the list will show `Start by adding a glass product to the job`. Glass cannot be assigned to panels with a glass product loaded.

## Setting the Product List

The Glass Tool Tab uses glass products to represent glass on an elevation. 

WinBidPro will include a number of glass options by default and gives you the ability to create, or copy and modify to meet your glass requirements.

* `Find Product` - Load a glass product to a job. Once a product is added you'll see it on the glass list in the Tool Tab.

* `Create Product` - Configure a new glass product. See [Custom Glass Products](../custom-parts/glass-products) for more details.

* `Show Assignments` - Toggle a list of glass products assigned to the current elevation. 
    * If no products are assigned to panels, the list will show `No lites have been assigned a product` while this list is active.

### Modifying Glass Products

The product list lets you change certain values of the glass product as it appears in this job. These options are covered in [Configuring Glass] but here's a couple options that can be really helpful when representing different kinds of glass:

* `Fill Pattern` - Change the fill / hatch pattern drawn on the surface of the glass.

* `Draw Color` - Change the color of the glass.


## Assigning Glass

### Process

To assign glass, make sure a glass product is loaded and available from the list. Select the glass product you want to use, click `Add`, and then click the panels you want to apply it to.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/tool-tabs/editor-glass.gif"/></div>

### Options

* `Add` - Assign the current glass product to the panels you click.

* `Delete` - Remove glass product from the panels you click.

There are also some options here to help speed up glass assignment:

* `By Lite` - Default option. `Add` or `Delete` glass on individual panels.

* `By Row` - `Add` or `Delete` glass for a full row of panels, including door glazing.
* `By Elevation` - `Add` or `Delete` glass for a to all panels in the elevation, including door glazing.

Once a glass product is applied to a panel, the preview will update to show where and what kind of glass is applied.

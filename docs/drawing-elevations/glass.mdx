Apply glass products to selected panels.

The Glass tool lists glass products loaded into the current job. Once applied, your panels will update to have product labels and any color/fill settings you specify for that product.

The list shows `Start by adding a glass product to the job` if no products are loaded. For glass counts to be reported, **glass products need to be applied to panels**.

## Setting the Product List

The Glass tool tab uses glass products to count glass in your elevation. 

* `Find Product` - Load a glass product to a job.

* `Create Product` - Configure a new glass product. See [Configuring Glass Products](../custom-parts/config-glass) for more details.

* `Show Assignments` - Toggle a list of glass assignments in the current elevation.

### Modifying Glass Products

Using the product list, you can make adhoc changes to glass products loaded into the job by double-clicking the values.


:::tip
    You can use these options to help identify glass assignments in the elevation:

    * `Fill Pattern` - Change the fill / hatch pattern drawn on the surface of the glass.

    * `Draw Color` - Change the color of the glass.
:::


## Assigning Glass

### Process

To assign glass, make sure a glass product is loaded. Select the product, click `Add`, and click panels to apply it.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/tool-tabs/editor-glass.gif"/></div>

### Options

* `Add` - Assign the current glass product to the panels you click.

* `Delete` - Remove glass product from the panels you click.

Use these settings to speed up assignment:

* `By Lite` - Default option. `Add` or `Delete` glass to individual panels.

* `By Row` - `Add` or `Delete` glass to a full row of panels.
* `By Elevation` - `Add` or `Delete` glass to all panels in the elevation.

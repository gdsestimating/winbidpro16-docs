Once you've [finalized your parts and estimate settings](../finalize), you can turn your parts lists into a variety of print-ready deliverables. 

Each of these is found in the bottom half of the `Reports` toolbar marked with green icons. 

## Shared Options

* `Format of Imperial Measurements` - A drop menu to select measurement style with options for:

    * `Fractional` - For example -- 1 1/2"

    * `Decimal` - For example -- 1.5"

* ☑ `Show Pricing` / ☑`Include Price` - Toggle `Total Price` column.

## All Reports & Settings

Click this option to quickly change report settings and export multiple of them at once. Click the formats you want to export for each report to toggle them on / off, then click `Run Reports` to export them.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/all-reports-csv.png"/></div>

### Options

#### Reports Tab

* `Base Save Location` - Specify or change where you want your reports to be saved on your computer.

* `Print` - Click to mark this report for download as a print-ready version.

    * Each `Print` button also has a `>` button next to it. Click for quick-access options like print orientation and report settings. 

* `CSV` - Click to mark this report for download as a CSV file for use in spreadsheet minis. 
    
    * See [Export to CSV / Spreadsheets](./spreadsheets) for more details.

* `Run Reports` - Generates prints / CSV downloads for all marked reports.

    * `Skip Report Preview` - Toggle to skip the preview step of downloading these reports.

## Job Reports

These cover print-ready deliverables for your job as a whole.

### Final Parts

A full list of parts in the job in a clean, printable report.

* This report is generated from the [Final Parts](../finalize/#final-parts) list.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-final-parts.png"/></div>

#### Options

* ☑ `By Package` - Toggle `Units/Pkg`, `Total Pkgs` and `Price/Pkg` columns. If toggled off, `Price/Pkg` column is replaced with `Price/Unit`.

### Final Glass

A full list of glass products applied to panels in the job in a clean, printable report.

* This report is generated from the [Glass](../finalize/#glass) summary.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-final-glass.png"/></div>

### Cut List

A full list of extrusion cuts in the job in a clean, printable report.

* This report is generated from the [Cut List](../finalize/#cut-list) optimized part summary.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-cut-list.png"/></div>

#### Options

* ☑ `Show Drops` - Toggle showing clean cut drops.

### Print Cut List & Glass Labels

Build and print your cut list and glass list in a label-friendly format.

* See [Cut List and Glass Labels](./labels) for more details.

* These labels are generated from the [Cut List](../finalize/#cut-list) optimized part summary, and the [Glass](../finalize/#glass) summary.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-labels-1.png"/></div>

### Glass to Order

A full list of glass products applied to panels in the job in a clean, printable order form.

* This report is generated from the [Glass](../finalize/#glass) summary.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-glass-order.png"/></div>

#### Options

* `Location Format` - Drop down with the following options:

    * `Elev Mark + GDS Location Code` - Location is listed as the elevation and the unique glass marker. For Example: SF-1-g1#1

    * `Elev Mark only` - Location shown as the elevation only. For Example: SF-1

### Glass Quote Request

A full list of glass products applied to panels in the job in a clean, printable quote request.

* This report is generated from the [Glass](../finalize/#glass) summary.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-glass-quote.png"/></div>

### Lineal Parts

## Elevation Reports

These cover print-ready deliverables for your job, split up per elevation.

### Parts

A breakdown of parts per elevation in a clean, printable format.

* This report is generated from the [Final Parts](../finalize/#final-parts) list as the parts appear in each elevation.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-elevation-parts.png"/></div>

#### Options

* `Primary Sort` / `Secondary Sort` - A drop menu to sort parts with the following options:

    * `Part #`

    * `Type/Component`

### Glass

A breakdown of glass products applied to panels per elevation in a clean, printable report.

* This report is generated from the [Glass](../finalize/#glass) summary as glass appears in each elevation.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-elevation-glass.png"/></div>


### Cost

A breakdown of cost per elevation in a clean, printable format.

* This report is generated from the [Final Parts](../finalize/#final-parts) list and [Markup](../finalize/#markup) settings as they affect pricing per elevation.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-elevation-cost.png"/></div>

### Labor

A breakdown of labor per elevation in a clean, printable format.

* This report is generated from the [Labor Breakdown](../finalize/#labor-breakdown) per elevation.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/print-elevation-labor.png"/></div>

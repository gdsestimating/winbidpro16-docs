The shop drawings output of WinBidPro takes your elevations and the details of your assigned framing systems, and generates a drawing set for your job that you can print or export to CAD-friendly file types.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/sample-shop-drawings.png"/></div>

## Page Setup

Use this section to set how your pages are printed when pressing the `Print All` or `Print Selected Page(s)` options.

:::tip
    When using a title block, you'll want to make sure the it's meant for your `Page Size` and `Orientation` to avoid too much white space or cropped out information.

    Title blocks provided by WinBidPro are named based on their size and orientation. For example: 
    
    * '16-TitleBlock-8.5x11-Landscape.dwg` is a v16 title block meant for a letter-sized print (8 1/2" x 11") in landscape orientation.
:::

* ☑ `Use different layer settings for shop drawings` - Change which layer settings to use for your shop drawings. 

    * See the [Layer Settings](../getting-started/interface/sidebar#layer-settings) for more details.

## Elevation Sheet Settings

* `Elevation Scale` - Set the scale of elevations on your sheets.

* `Elevation Distribution` - Select how many elevations should show per sheet. Options include:

    * `1 Per Sheet` - Forces only one elevation per sheet, regardless of size and spacing.

    * `Multiple Per Sheet` - Will add multiple elevations onto the same sheet when there is enough space.
    
        * `Elevation Spacing` - Amount of white space to put between your elevations, measured in inches. 

* ☑ `Show Panel Numbers` - Toggles WinBidPro panel identifiers.

* ☑ `Show Glass` - Toggles typical hatch patterns for glass material.

* ☑ `Include Title Block` - Toggles the selected title block on all sheets.

* `Title Block File` - Sets the title block to use with your shop drawings.

* `Padding` - The spacing around the elevations measured in inches. Use this to change where the elevations sit to adjust to different title blocks.

    * `T` - Top - padding in inches above the elevation.

    * `B` - Bottom - padding in inches below the elevation.
    
    * `L` - Left - padding in inches left of the elevation.
    
    * `R` - Right - padding in inches right of the elevation.


* `Edit Title Block Attributes` - Opens the Title Block attributes window.

    * See the [Title Blocks](./title-blocks) doc for more details.

## Detail Sheet Settings

* ☑ `Include Details` - Toggle including detail sheets for your assigned framing systems.

* `Scale` - Set the scale of frame details.

* `Spacing` - Set the spacing between details on your sheets, measured in inches.

## Cover Page

* ☑ `Include Cover Page` - Toggle generating a cover page with your sheets.

* `Edit Cover Page` - Opens the Cover Page configuration window. 
    
    * See the [Cover Page](./cover-page) doc for more details.

## Output

* `Generate Pages` - Click this button to generate the pages after settings all the desired options.

:::tip
    Changes made to any settings will keep your other settings intact, but you'll clear any generated pages and you'll need to to click `Generate Pages` again. 
    
    It's best to set all your options before you click this button to avoid having to regenerate.
:::

* `Export to DWG / Export to DXF` - After your pages are generated, you can export them to CAD-friendly files .DWG and .DXF.

* `Print All` / `Print Selected Page(s)` - After your pages are generated, you can send them to your printer or print to .PDF based on your settings in `Page Setup` (see above).

* `Close` - Click to exit this window.

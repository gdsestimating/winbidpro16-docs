Outputs from WinBidPro fall under two general categories: Printed Reports and Shop Drawings.

See below for a quick description of each. Click each topic's page on the left for more details.

## Printed Reports

Printed reports are shown with green icon in the `Reports` toolbar menu. These cover your lists, spreadsheets, labels, and job summaries to be shared or printed outside of WinBidPro.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/sample-printed-report.png"/></div>


## Shop Drawings

Shop drawing options are listed under the `Drawings` toolbar menu. This covers shop drawings and frame details of your elevations to be exported as a PDF drawing set or CAD file.

WinBidPro also includes configurable cover pages and title blocks for a clean, professional, and consistent drawing set.

:::info 
    This section covers how to review your job and modify your parts lists and settings to prep them for print-ready deliverables. 
:::

---

Job summaries, generated parts lists, and proposal settings are found in the top half of the `Reports` toolbar menu. These use black icons to help them stand out from the exportable versions. Use these reports to review and dial in your proposal before exporting.

For details on the exportable versions (the green icons in `Reports`), see [Exports & Print Outs](../output/).

## Shared Options

Most of these lists have a shared set of options detailed here:

* Grouping - Break your lists up into sections.

    * See [List Groups & Filters](../features-and-tips/filters) for more details.
    
    * <img src="/screenshots/reports/grouping-options.png"/>`Group Options` - Make quick changes to all groups:
        
        * `Expand All Groups`
        * `Collapse All Groups`
        * `Delete All Groups`

* Filtering - Limit your lists to items meeting specific conditions.
    
    * See [List Groups & Filters](../features-and-tips/filters) for more details.

    * <img src="/screenshots/reports/columns.png"/> `Column Chooser` - A list of all columns and with an option to ☑ toggle them on or off.
    
* <img src="/screenshots/reports/export-icon.png"/> `Export to CSV` - An export option for use in spreadsheet apps like Excel. 
    
    * See [Exporting CSV / Spreadsheets](../output/spreadsheets) for more details.
    
* <img src="/screenshots/reports/printer-icon.png"/> `Print` - Quick access to the print-ready versions of this list.
    
    * See [Printed Reports](../output/reports) for more details.

## Reports

### Final Parts 
List of all parts and their counts, pricing, and other attributes. 

This list is generated from your elevations, based on the framing system configurations applied to them.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/final-parts.png"/></div>

#### Options
* Manual Edits - You can use the <img src="/screenshots/reports/add-icon.png"/> `Add Part`, <img src="/screenshots/reports/trash-icon.png"/> `Delete Part`, and <img src="/screenshots/reports/undo-icon.png"/> `Undo` buttons to make manual changes to the Final Parts list. 
    
    * See [Manually Changing Parts Lists](./manual-parts) for more details.

* ☑ `Show Unit Pricing` - Toggle between per-unit and per-package pricing and totals.

* `Change Multipliers...` - Apply a count multiplier to all your part totals at once.
    
:::tip
    `Change Multipliers...` is applied only to currently visible parts. To apply this to a select group of parts, [filter](../features-and-tips/filters) your list before using this feature.

    The change this for individual parts, you can manually type it in under the `Multiplier` column.
::: 


### Glass 
List of all panels with [Glass Products](../custom-parts/glass-products) applied. In the top right corner, you'll see a summary of your `Total Glass Square Ft` and the `Total Price`.
 

This is a review-only list. To make changes to it, edit your glass products and where they're applied to your elevations.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/final-glass.png"/></div>


### Cut List 
Pre-optimized list of all extrusion cuts in the current job.

This list is generated from your elevations, based on the framing system configurations applied to them.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/cut-list.png"/></div>

#### Options

* Manual Edits - You can use the <img src="/screenshots/reports/add-icon.png"/> `Add Part`, <img src="/screenshots/reports/trash-icon.png"/> `Delete Part`, and <img src="/screenshots/reports/undo-icon.png"/> `Undo` buttons to make manual changes to the Cut List. 
    
    * See [Manually Changing Parts Lists](./manual-parts) for more details.

* ☑ `Active Elevation` - A toggle to quickly filter out any cuts not in the active elevation.

* <img src="/screenshots/reports/split-screen.png"/> `Split Screen` - Splits the main window between the cut list and the CAD view of the active elevation.

    * <img src="/screenshots/reports/link-toggles.png"/> `Split Screen Link` - Toggle on to sync `Active Elevation` and `Split Screen` so both are turned on / off together.

### Stock List

This is a review-only list. To make changes to it, edit your cut list and elevations, or modify your framing systems.

While the parts summaries are generating your base part costs, the `Markup` window in the `Reports` tab is the main place you'll go to dial in your estimate pricing.

You can also find a handful of quick job detail settings here as they'll show up on your `Final Summary`.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/markup.png"/></div>

#### Tool Bar

* <img src="/screenshots/reports/printer-icon.png"/> `Print` - Quick access to the print-ready versions of this list with the following options:

    * Print Contractor Report

    * Print Customer Report

* ☑ `Do Not Tax Markup Amount` / ☑ `Do Not Markup Surcharge Amount` - Toggle including the Markup or Surcharge amount in your tax calculation.

* `Save to Defaults` - Save the Markup options and rates set here as a default for all jobs.

* `Restore from Defaults` - Restore all options and rates to the default setting for your jobs.

## Details

The details section on the left side of the Markup window are there to include commonly used job details to your report.

### Quote Details

* `Job` - Name of the job. Can be changed in the `Job Details` option of the `Job` toolbar menu.

* `Quote Number` - The unique number your company will use for this quote.

* `Dates` - Commonly listed dates you can add to your report.

### Estimator Headers

Manually fill in estimator details, or select from your `Contact List` found in the `Catalog` toolbar menu. 

### Contractor Headers

Manually fill in contractor details, or select from your `Contact List` found in the `Catalog` toolbar menu. 

### Perimeter & Square Footage

These values are automatically updated based on your changes made here and in your elevations.

* ☑ `Include in contractor report` - Toggle including this breakdown in the contractor report print out.

### Markup and Surcharge Totals

These values are automatically updated based on your changes made in your markups and surcharges in this window.

* ☑ `Include in contractor report` - Toggle including this breakdown in the contractor report print out.


## Pricing

Pricing is split up into `Material`, `Labor` and `Miscellaneous Charges` with each of these split into `Price`, `Rate` and `Total`. Where ☑ toggles are shown next to the each entry, you can click them to overwrite the amount shown and type in your own.

* `Price` - a base price generated from the parts pricing and counts or overwritten here.

* `Rate` - a percentage you can add to the base prices. 

    * Tax is a rate added near the end of each section.

* `Total` - a generated amount based on the `Price` and `Rate`

## Notes

You can use the `Sections Bid`, `Exclusions`, and `Notes` sections to include extra details and comments to your reports.

While the other reports are generating your base costs, the `Markup` window is where you can dial in your rates and estimate.

You can also find a summary of most of your job details, like dates and stakeholder information, and change them all in one spot.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/reports/markup.png"/></div>

#### Tool Bar

* <img src="/screenshots/reports/printer-icon.png"/> `Print` - Quick access to the print-ready versions of this list with the following options:

    * Print Contractor Report - Includes all details on this page.

    * Print Customer Report - Rolls markup amounts into the estimate without showing details.

* ☑ `Do Not Tax Markup Amount` / ☑ `Do Not Markup Surcharge Amount` - Toggle including Markup or Surcharge amount in your tax calculation.

* `Save to Defaults` - Save the Markup options and rates set here as a default for all jobs.

* `Restore from Defaults` - Restore all options and rates to the default setting for your jobs.

## Details

The details section on the left adds some job information to your print outs.

### Quote Details

* `Job` - Name of the job. You can change this value from `Job` > `Job Details` in the main menu.

* `Quote Number` - The unique number your company will use for this quote.

* `Dates` - Commonly listed dates you can add to your report.

### Estimator Headers

Manually fill in estimator details, or select one from `Catalog` > `Contact List`. 

### Contractor Headers

Manually fill in contractor details, or select one from `Catalog` > `Contact List`. 

### Perimeter & Square Footage

These values are automatically updated based on changes made here and in the elevations.

* ☑ `Include in contractor report` - Toggle including this breakdown in the contractor report.

### Markup and Surcharge Totals

These values are automatically updated based on changes made to your markups and surcharges.

* ☑ `Include in contractor report` - Toggle including this breakdown in the contractor report.


## Pricing

Pricing is split up into `Material`, `Labor` and `Miscellaneous Charges` with each of these has `Price`, `Rate` and `Total` columns. 

When ☑ toggles are shown, you can click these to overwrite the value with a manual entry.

* `Price` - A base price generated from the parts pricing and counts or overwritten here.

* `Rate` - A percentage you can add to the base prices. 

    * Tax is a rate added near the end of each section and always visible in reports.

* `Total` - A generated amount based on the `Price` and `Rate`.

## Notes

You can use the `Sections Bid`, `Exclusions`, and `Notes` boxes to include extra details or comments to your report.

:::info 
    This section covers the optimized parts lists, generated summaries, and settings for your active job. 
    
    After you [Draw Your Elevations](../drawing-elevations/), these are where you review and refine your estimate and prep it for your final [Output](../output/).
:::

---

Optimizers are your part lists, cut lists, products, labor settings, etc. in WinBidPro. These are the black icons in the `Reports` toolbar menu.

Each of these is a working total of the parts, pieces, and settings tracked by WinBidPro for your current job. These are also where you'll find options to help dial in your estimate. 

For finalized, exportable versions of these lists, see the [Printed Reports](../output/reports) page. The print-ready versions of these reports use a green icon in the `Reports` toolbar menu to help them stand out from your optimizers.

## Shared Options

Most optimized lists have a shared set of options detailed here:

* Grouping - Break your lists up into sections based on the values in specific columns. 
    
    * See [List Groups & Filters](../features-and-tips/filters) for more details.

    * <img src="/screenshots/opt/grouping-options.png"/>`Group Options` - A menu of quick options to `Expand All Groups`, `Collapse All Groups`, or `Delete All Groups`

* Filtering -  Limit your list down to entries that meet specific conditions.
    
    * See [List Groups & Filters](../features-and-tips/filters) for more details.

    * <img src="/screenshots/opt/columns.png"/> `Column Chooser` - A list of all columns and with ☑ toggles to show / hide each one. 
    
* <img src="/screenshots/opt/export-icon.png"/> `Export to CSV` - An export for use in spreadsheet apps like Excel. 
    
    * See [Exporting CSV / Spreadsheets](../output/spreadsheets) for more details.
    
* <img src="/screenshots/opt/printer-icon.png"/> `Print` - Quick access to the printed report version of this list.
    
    * See [Printed Reports](../output/reports) for more details.

## Optimizers

### Final Parts 
List of all parts and their counts, pricing, and other attributes. 

This list is generated from the parts needed to build your [drawn elevations](../drawing-elevations/), based on the framing systems applied to them.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/opt/final-parts.png"/></div>

#### Options
* Manual Edits - You can use the <img src="/screenshots/opt/add-icon.png"/> `Add Part`, <img src="/screenshots/opt/trash-icon.png"/> `Delete Part`, and <img src="/screenshots/opt/undo-icon.png"/> `Undo` buttons to make manual changes to the Final Parts list. 
    
    * See [Manually Changing Parts Lists](./manual-parts) for more details.

* ☑ `Show Unit Pricing` - Toggle between per-units or per-package pricing and totals.

* `Change Multipliers...` - Apply a count multiplier to part totals across the entire visible list at once.
    
:::tip
    `Change Multipliers...` is applied only to currently visible parts. To apply this to a select group of parts, [filter](../features-and-tips/filters) your list before using this feature.

    You can also change the multiplier for individual parts by manually changing the value set in the `Multiplier` column.
::: 


### Glass 
A summary of all panels with [Glass Products](../custom-parts/glass-products) applied to them in your elevations. If no glass product is applied to panels, they won't be counted on this list or effect the totaled square footage.

In the top right corner, you'll see your `Total Glass Square Ft` and the `Total Price` based on the pricing applied to your glass products.

This list is meant only for review. The counts here are automatically generated and can't be changed directly from here. To change these values, make changes to the Glass Products or your elevations.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/opt/final-glass.png"/></div>


### Cut List 
Pre-optimized list of all extrusion cuts in the current job.

This list is generated from the parts needed to build your [drawn elevations](../drawing-elevations/), based on the framing systems applied to them.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/opt/cut-list.png"/></div>

#### Options

* Manual Edits - You can use the <img src="/screenshots/opt/add-icon.png"/> `Add Part`, <img src="/screenshots/opt/trash-icon.png"/> `Delete Part`, and <img src="/screenshots/opt/undo-icon.png"/> `Undo` buttons to make manual changes to the Cut List. 
    
    * See [Manually Changing Parts Lists](./manual-parts) for more details.

* ☑ `Active Elevation` - A quick-filter toggle unique to this cut list that will filter out any cuts not in the active elevation.

* <img src="/screenshots/opt/split-screen.png"/> `Split Screen` - Splits the job window so it's shared by the cut list and the CAD view for the active elevation.

    * <img src="/screenshots/opt/link-toggles.png"/> `Split Screen Link` - Toggle synced `Active Elevation` and `Split Screen` toggles so that both are activated / deactivated with one click. Reduces the amount of clicks needed to switch between the full cut list and the cuts for an individual elevation when reviewing your cut list.

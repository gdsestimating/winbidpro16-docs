Optimizers are you part lists, cut lists, products, labor settings, etc. in WinBidPro. These are the black icons in the `Reports` toolbar menu.

For printing reports, reports and labels not intended for collaborators not using WinBidPro, see [Printed Reports](../output/reports). These reports use a green icon in the `Reports` toolbar menu.

## Optimizers

* `Final Parts`

* `Glass`
* `Cut List` 
* `Stock List`
* `Markup`
* `Operable Windows`
*  `Doors` - A list of entrances in the current job, offering a searchable and filterable spreadsheet of all entrances.
* `Elevations Overview` - An expanded version of the list of elevations in the current job found in the Job Explorer, offering a searchable and filterable spreadsheet of all the elevations and their more important characteristics.
* `Labor Breakdown`

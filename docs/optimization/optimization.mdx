:::info 
    This section covers the optimized summaries and settings for your active job. 
    
    After you [Draw Your Elevations](../drawing-elevations/), these are where you review and refine your estimate, and prep it for your final [Output](../output/).
:::

---

Optimizers are your part lists, cut lists, products, labor settings, etc. in WinBidPro. These are the black icons in the `Reports` toolbar menu. 

Each of these gives you working totals of all parts, pieces, and settings that effect your estimate, and gives you options to help dial it in. 

For finalized, exportable versions of these, see the [Printed Reports](../output/reports) section. These reports use a green icon in the `Reports` toolbar menu to help them stand out from optimizers.

:::tip
    Most optimized lists can be exported for use in spreadsheet apps -- see [Exporting CSV / Spreadsheets](../output/spreadsheets) for more details.
:::

## Optimizers

You can find a brief description of each optimizer here. Click each topic's page on the left for more details.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/sample-optimizer.png"/></div>


:::tip
    For more details on how to organize your lists or find specific entries, see [List Groups & Filters](../features-and-tips/filters).
:::


* `Final Parts` - List of all parts and their counts.

* `Glass` - List of all glass products and glazed areas applied to panels.

* `Cut List` - Pre-optimized list of all extrusion cuts.

* `Stock List` - Pre-optimized list of stock packages.

* `Markup` - Settings to dial in the final price of your estimate.

* `Operable Windows` - A list of all operable window applied to panels.

*  `Doors` - A list of all configured entrances applied.

* `Elevations Overview` - An expanded list of all elevations and their details.

* `Labor Breakdown` - A list of all applied labor sets and their totals.

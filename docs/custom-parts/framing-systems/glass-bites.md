You can apply a default glass bite to your framing system in the settings tab, and then apply specific bites to individual assemblies in the component assemblies.

## Default Bites

`Glass Bites` option in the `Settings` tab of your framing system are the default glass bites. Unless these are overwritten by specific assemblies, these will apply across your entire system.

* L/R are the left and right bites in inches.
* T/B are the top and bottom bites in inches.

## Component Assemblies

You can find glass bites under `Component Properties` when editing a component assembly where glass bites are present.

## At Transoms

Entrances which require transom stops/sash can have these settings configured in the Door Frame editor. 

You can access this view from the Entrances tool tab, by following these steps:

1. In the example below, the entrance needs sash applied to the door jambs and door header of the transom opening.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/transom-glass-unaltered.png"/></div>
<br/>

2. Switch to the `Entrances` tool tab and open the `Door Frame` editor view.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/transom-glass-add-stop.png"/></div>
<br/>

3. Next to `Transom Stops`, you can open any of the drop down menus and click `Add New` to create a new transom stop configuration. Once this is configured, you can use this same setting for the other edges as well.

<div class="app-img"><img src="/screenshots/transom-glass-new-sash.png"/></div>
<br/>

4. Once you've created and applied the transom stop configurations for your entrance, click `Save` to apply them to the elevation drawing. By default, these stops will show up using green lines where applied.

<div class="mini-img"><img src="/screenshots/transom-glass-applied-stops.png"/></div>
